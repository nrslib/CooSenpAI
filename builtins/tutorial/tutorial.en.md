## watch-intro
Finally, Vision AI and Hearing AI. Click this guide to open the window. Press “Vision” at the top to let me look at your screen occasionally and speak up only when useful. You can choose specific apps, the whole screen, or both. Press “Hearing” next to it to transcribe speaker and microphone audio and use it in the same way. One important note: while Vision AI or Hearing AI is ON, what appears on your screen or what you hear is sent to the AI. Turn it off before opening passwords or anything private. This is still the tutorial, so your screen is not sent to the AI yet. Feel free to try turning it on.
